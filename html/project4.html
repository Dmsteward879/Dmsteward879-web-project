My Gaming Universe

The All-Time Greats

Gaming has been a lifelong passion of mine; ever since I was a child, I have been fascinated by it. I love exploring rich worlds, solving complex puzzles, playing strategy games, and the thrill of defeating strong, scary monsters. Below are the three franchises that define my gaming experience.

My Favorite Series

  • Resident Evil: Known as the face of survival horror, this series is all about tension and resource management. Players typically find themselves in claustrophobic environments inhabited by zombies and mutated bioweapons, where they must solve intricate puzzles while carefully conserving every bullet to stay alive.
  • Final Fantasy: An anthology of epic role-playing games where every mainline entry features a completely new world and cast of characters. While the stories range from medieval fantasy to cyberpunk, they all center on a group of unlikely heroes banding together to fight a great evil while exploring deep themes of friendship, fate, and internal struggle.
  • Pokémon: A world-famous creature-collector series where players take on the role of a "Trainer." The core adventure involves exploring vibrant regions to catch unique species, training them through turn-based battles, and defeating Gym Leaders to eventually become the region's Pokémon League Champion.
