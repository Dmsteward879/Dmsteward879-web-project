Pixels and Polygons: My Retro Journey

The Allure of Retro Gaming

There is something magical about the simplicity and challenge of classic video games. While modern graphics are impressive, I am a sucker for the charm of 8-bit and 16-bit adventures.

I find that retro games often rely on tight gameplay loops and memorable music to keep players engaged, creating an experience that stands the test of time.

Favorite Genres

The Thrill of Horror

My absolute favorite genre is horror. Whether it's the fixed camera angles of classic survival horror or the eerie atmosphere of pixelated psychological thrillers, I love a game that can make me jump.

RPGs: A Break from the Scares

From time to time, I enjoy diving into a deep RPG. Building a character and exploring a vast, story-driven world provides a perfect change of pace from the intensity of horror games.

My Retro Essentials

  • Classic Survival Horror Titles
  • Turn-Based RPGs
  • CRT Monitors and Original Hardware
