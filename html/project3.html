Enter the Survival Horror: Resident Evil

My Favorite Series: Resident Evil

Ever since I stepped into the Spencer Mansion, I have been hooked on Resident Evil. The series perfectly captures the tension of being trapped in a nightmare with limited resources.

I love how the games evolved from fixed camera angles and "tank controls" to the immersive over-the-shoulder perspectives we see in the modern remakes.

The Horror and the RPG Elements

Atmospheric Dread

The horror in Resident Evil isn't just about jump scares; it's about the atmosphere. Walking down a quiet hallway with only a few bullets left creates a unique kind of stress that I love.

Inventory Management (RPG Lite)

As a fan of RPGs, I actually enjoy the inventory management in these games. Deciding whether to carry an extra green herb or a key item feels like a tactical puzzle that adds depth to the experience.

Resident Evil Essentials

  • Classic Zombies and Bio-Organic Weapons
  • The Iconic Umbrella Corporation Lore
  • Strategic Puzzle Solving
